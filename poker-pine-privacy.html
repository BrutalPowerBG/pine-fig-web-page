PokerPine Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2024

PokerPine is a mobile application developed by PineFig. We respect the privacy of our users and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information We Collect

When using the application, we may collect the following information:

  • Email address — used to create and manage a user account via Firebase Authentication
  • Technical information such as:
    • Device type
    • Operating system version
    • Usage data (e.g., crashes and performance)

2. How We Use the Information

The collected information is used to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts
  • Provide access to application features
  • Improve stability and performance
  • Resolve technical issues

3. Data Storage and Processing

Data is processed and stored through third-party services, including:

  • Firebase Authentication (a service provided by Google)

These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies. See also Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties, except when necessary to provide the service (e.g., via Firebase).

5. Children

PokerPine is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that such data has been provided, it will be deleted.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, including the use of secure third-party services such as Firebase.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

8. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at:

contact@pinefig.com
